These files are UL material: they're fine in this private repo, but the app never displays them and they're never published.

When two versions of a document disagree, the most recent one wins. Differences are noted below.

## Differences between documents

- **Interim report deadline.** Weeks 1 and 2 give 25/10/2026, and the proposal form's milestones use the same date. Week 3 updates it to **06/11/2026** (and the proposal form to 08/10/2026). 06/11/2026 is current.
- **Final report deadline.** None of these documents gives a date. Week 1 says the final report is submitted as part of CS4448 Residency 4. The date 07/04/2027 comes from `PLAN.md` and still needs a course source.
- **Ethics lead time.** The spec says around 4–6 weeks but it can take 6–8. The proposal form says four to six weeks. Plan for 6–8. Week 1 adds that the committee deadline is the first Thursday of each month, and it doesn't meet in August.
- **Weighting.** Week 1 gives the proposal form 10% and the interim report 90% of CS4437. The final report is marked under CS4448.

## Points from these documents that the plan doesn't mention

- The spec's marking guide has a seventh category, **Research paper quality (15)**, covering work done to date, organisation and presentation, and references and citations. It applies to the whole paper, so it has no section ID in `PLAN.md` §3.5.
- The video summary is marked together with the conclusion (10 marks in total).
- Word limit: 10,000 words maximum, **including the reference list**. Going more than 10% over is penalised.
- Week 2: writing in the third person ("This report will explore" rather than "I will"). Agree this with the supervisor.
- The spec requires an academic integrity and plagiarism declaration with both the interim and final submissions.

## Question
Whether any part involves human participants (VAL-02 labelling, VAL-03 evaluators), and if so, the UL Science & Engineering expedited application.

Timing: the lead time is around 4–6 weeks but can take 6–8 (FYP specification). The committee deadline is the first Thursday of each month, and the committee doesn't meet in August (FYP week 1 slides).

## Options considered
- **Option A**: no human participants, so no ethics application. The author labels the gap set and any medical sample, and carries out the usability evaluation alone. **Chosen.**
- Other options (an expedited application to recruit labellers or usability participants) were ruled out because of the 4 to 8 week approval wait.

## Decision
**Option A, decided 2 November 2026.** No human participants, so no ethics application. The gap set (VAL-01), and the medical sample if it is added later (VAL-02), are written and labelled by the author, who also carries out the usability evaluation (VAL-03).

## Why
- It keeps the project on schedule with no 4 to 8 week approval wait.
- Every data source is public text with no patients involved.

## Consequences
- Get written confirmation from the supervisor that no application is needed, since researchers cannot exempt their own projects. This is needed **before labelling starts**.
- The gap set (VAL-01) and any medical sample (VAL-02) have one labeller, so agreement comes from re-labelling a subset after a gap (intra-rater agreement), not from other people.
- VAL-03 uses readability formulas and the author's own heuristic evaluation and walkthrough. One evaluator finds only part of the problems (Nielsen and Molich, 1990), which goes in the threats to validity.
- Medical text comes from public sources only: no clinical notes or hospital data.
- Harmful examples in the paper get a content warning and placeholders (Kirk et al., 2022).
- The Methodology section states this position and the reasons.
